Introduction

Traffic Way Live

My website will be for a concert venue in SLO County. For now I'm naming it "Traffic Way Live" and having its location be on Traffic Way in Atascadero, near where the venue Dark Nectar used to be before it closed down.

Target audience

The target audience for customers and attendees of this venue are young people with alternative-electronic music taste. These are most likely Cal Poly students or any locals around the same age, primarily Gen Z through older Millennials. It will host and showcase artists who are more underground and don't typically play in this area because of how our local venues are more suitable for other styles of music (It will be more a hall/room than a theater or outdoor arena).

This website will provide information on upcoming shows and be the main way to purchase tickets to the events hosted. It will also provide important information on the business itself such as the address and contact information.

About

Traffic Way Live brings new, forward-thinking alternative artists to SLO County music fanatics and curious casuals alike. We opened our doors in 2017 with the dream of showcasing artists who push boundaries in electronic music who may not have had a space to perform in the SLO area before. As the years have gone on, we have welcomed a wide variety of music acts to our stage and continue to bring the best to our SLO music community.
