Traffic Way Live is a home for forward-thinking electronic music, experimental sounds, and artists pushing the boundaries of what's possible. Located in Atascadero, we bring exciting emerging and established artists to the Central Coast, connecting local music fanatics and curious newcomers with sounds and experiences they might not find anywhere else in the area.

We opened our doors in 2017 with a vision to create a space for artists exploring the more experimental, underground, and unconventional corners of electronic music, highlighting artists who may not have otherwise had an opportunity to perform in the SLO area. Since then, we've continued to welcome a diverse range of DJs, producers, and performers, showcasing everything from cutting-edge electronic production and underground dance music to experimental pop and genre-blurring sounds.

At our core, we're driven by discovery. We believe live music should be an opportunity to experience something unexpected, explore new sounds, and connect with artists pushing creative boundaries. We're proud to be part of the San Luis Obispo County music community and remain committed to bringing exciting, independent, and forward-thinking electronic music to the Central Coast. Whether you're deeply immersed in electronic music or simply curious about what's out there, there's always something new to discover at Traffic Way Live.
